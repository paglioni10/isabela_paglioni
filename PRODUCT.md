# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** women and families in Florianópolis and region who want their home organized — closets, kitchens, pantries, laundry, china cabinets, home offices. They usually arrive from Instagram or a referral, on a phone, deciding whether to trust someone with their private spaces.
- **Life-transition clients:** people expecting a baby (nursery and layette), newlyweds setting up a new home, and people moving (pre- and post-move).
- Secondary, not the focus of persuasion: inclusive organization clients and online consultancy clients from other cities.
- Content editor: Isabela herself, non-technical, updates the site through the Sanity panel at `/admin`.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for Isabela Paglioni, a professional organizer (personal organizer). Its job is to turn a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation to book a service. Success = WhatsApp contacts started from the site.

## Positioning

- **Lasting organization:** the goal is systems that are easy to maintain in daily life, not just a beautiful result on delivery day.
- **Proven results:** real project photos in the portfolio and video testimonials from real clients.
- Service is personal: Isabela herself serves and executes; contact goes straight to her WhatsApp.

## Operating Context

- Main conversion channel: WhatsApp (`wa.me/5548984863549`, with a pre-filled message). Secondary: Instagram `@isa.paglioniorganizer`, e-mail `isa.paglioni@hotmail.com`.
- Service area: Florianópolis and region, plus online consultancy.
- Visitors are mostly on mobile.
- Analytics: Microsoft Clarity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind + Framer Motion, deployed on Vercel (`isabela-paglioni.vercel.app`).
- Services, portfolio, testimonials, and tips come from Sanity (project `hw8ndejd`, dataset `production`); Isabela orders services and portfolio photos by drag and drop.
- Portfolio categories: Cozinha, Closet, Baby, Escritório, Mudança, Lavanderia, Cristaleira, Despensa.
- Services currently offered: Organização Residencial, Organização Baby, Recém-Casados, Pré e Pós-Mudança, Organização Inclusiva, Consultoria Online.
- Testimonials and tips are YouTube videos (mostly vertical Shorts).
- Site language: Brazilian Portuguese only.
- Undecided: pricing is not published; there is no booking system beyond WhatsApp.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Isa Paglioni" / Isabela Paglioni, "Personal Organizer".
- Tagline in use: "Organizando com propósito." Quote: "Organização é liberdade!"
- Voice: warm, first person, close and welcoming ("Terei o maior prazer…"), never corporate.

## Product Principles

1. Every section leads to the WhatsApp conversation; nothing competes with it.
2. Show real proof (photos, videos, numbers) instead of promises.
3. Communicate lasting, easy-to-maintain organization, not just a beautiful result on delivery day.
4. Mobile first: most visitors arrive from Instagram on their phone.
5. Isabela must be able to update everything herself in the panel, without code.
